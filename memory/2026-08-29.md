## Duplicate email to Rebeccah Sheridan (CGI/Dell) — root cause + tracker audit

Spencer flagged that Rebeccah Sheridan (CGI Interactive, Dell Technologies) got a reconnect email twice, and the tracker hadn't caught it — asked me to explain how it happened and re-audit the tracker for other people at risk of the same thing.

**Timeline reconstructed from Gmail:**
- **Aug 4** — Spencer sent Rebeccah a reconnect email ("Reaching out again — Spencer, VO for Dell/CGI"). She replied the same day, warmly: *"Thanks Spencer, great to hear from you! We'll keep you in mind for future projects in need of voiceover talent 😊"*
- That reply was never logged into the tracker at the time — no Status/Last-Contact update.
- **Aug 28** — the batch-of-10 "Old Client Reconnect" send (drafted from the ~18-name import list, cross-checked "against the live tracker") went out. The cross-check only looked at tracker rows, which still showed Rebeccah as unreached, so she got a second cold email ("Re: Dell Technologies VO") that never referenced her Aug 4 reply.
- **Aug 29 (today)** — apology sent in the *original* Aug 4 thread: "Sorry this message got lost! Looking forward to working with you again."

**Root cause:** the tracker cross-check before a batch send only reads its own rows, not the inbox. An inbound reply that never gets logged is invisible to that check, so a genuinely already-warm contact can get re-sent a cold reconnect.

**Fix applied:**
- Row 44 (Rebeccah) updated: Last Contact → 08/29/2026, Notes rewritten with the full root-cause + resolution, status confirmed as `Responded - Active`. No further outreach needed.
- Added a new hazard (#0) to `reference/outreach-pipeline.md` documenting this failure mode and the lesson: cross-check batch-send candidates against Gmail directly, not just the tracker.
- Audited the other 9 people in the same Aug 28 batch (Melissa Gillis, Evan Kay, Erin Daughenbaugh, Mel Kane, Lucas Bertoli, Taylor Ballam, Evan Romoff, Mackenzie Prokos, Devin Leisher) via direct Gmail search for pre-Aug-27 correspondence. All clean — either no prior contact, or the most recent thread was old and already closed out (6 months to 2 years stale), not a missed recent reply. **Rebeccah was the only duplicate in this batch.**

Tracker is confirmed accurate as of 2026-08-29.
